01. About me

I like building visually stunning designs that actually work. I started with interface design and front-end code, then went deeper into algorithms, data structures, and machine learning. I care about clean, usable products and getting the small details right.

I'm a third-year BSc Computer Science student at the University of Prince Edward Island. At Inalipa Inc., I designed UX/UI in Figma for a B2C platform, built front-end pages for a B2B website, and worked as a QA engineer on the B2C platform, where my testing helped cut bugs by about 20%. I'm now looking for internship roles where I can keep growing.

Outside class, I digital-paint characters and environments, draft game ideas, work on side projects and business ideas, and play third-person RPGs.

I grew up in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania, and now call Charlottetown a second home. I also volunteer with an East African community organization here.
