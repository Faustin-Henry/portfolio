About

Hi there! I’m Faustin, and I like building visually stunning designs that are functional. I started out designing interfaces and writing front-end code, and I’ve since gone deeper into algorithms, data structures, and machine learning. I care about clean, usable products and about getting the small details right. /n Right now, I’m a third-year BSc Computer Science student at the University of Prince Edward Island. Before that, I interned at Inalipa Inc., where I designed UX/UI in Figma for a B2CB platform, built front-end pages for a B2B website, and tested the B2C platform as a QA engineer, which helped cut bugs by about 20%. I’m looking for internship roles where I can keep growing and explore more in my field. \n Outside of class, you’ll find me digital-painting characters and environments, drafting game ideas, working on side projects and business ideas, or playing 3rd-person RPG games.
