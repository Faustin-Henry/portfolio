about

I'm a Computer Science student at the University of Prince Edward Island (expected 2028), originally from Tanzania and now based in Charlottetown, PE. My foundation is in data structures and algorithms, and my comparative programming languages course taught me to pick up new languages quickly.

I move between engineering and design: I test software for a living, design interfaces in Figma, and make digital art in my spare time.
